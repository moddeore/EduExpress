Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our platform.

How does the AI analysis work?

Our AI uses advanced natural language processing to analyze video content, titles, descriptions, and transcripts to determine how well each video covers your specified topics. It provides detailed coverage percentages and topic breakdowns.

Is the service free to use?

Yes! EduExpress is completely free to use. We believe in making quality educational content discovery accessible to everyone.

Can I search for videos in any language?

Currently, our AI analysis works best with English content. However, you can search for videos in any language, though the coverage analysis may be less accurate for non-English content.

How accurate is the topic coverage analysis?

Our AI analysis achieves high accuracy by analyzing multiple aspects of video content. While no system is perfect, our coverage percentages provide reliable guidance for finding relevant educational content.

Can I suggest new features?

Absolutely! We love hearing from our users. You can submit feature requests through this contact form or by emailing us directly. We review all suggestions and implement the most popular ones.
