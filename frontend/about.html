Our Story

From a simple idea to a revolutionary platform that's changing how people learn.

May 2025

The Idea

I noticed that students were spending hours searching for the right educational videos. They envisioned an AI-powered solution that could instantly find the most relevant content.

June 2025

Development Begins

Learning phase. My focus was on creating accurate topic analysis and coverage scoring algorithms.

July 2025

Prototype

Design the user interface and experience. Built a functional prototype and began user testing to gather feedback and refine the platform.

Oct 2025

Public Launch

EduExpress went public, serving learners worldwide. We integrated advanced machine learning models and expanded our video database to include millions of educational content.

Today

Continuous Innovation

We continue to improve our AI algorithms, add new features, and expand our reach. Our goal is to make quality education accessible to every learner on the planet.
